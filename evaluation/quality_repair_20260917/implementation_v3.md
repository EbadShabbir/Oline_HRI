# Third development candidate

This iteration follows the preserved interrupted second replay. Twelve cases
were recorded: eleven completed and one failed at the device RAM floor; four
were unattempted. Both independent reviewers passed nine recorded answers.

The supplied-plan validator now accepts explicit totals introduced by
"totaling", "totalling" or "totals". The second replay's first answer retained
the correct allocations and overall time, but the prior validator counted that
total as another step duration and withheld it. Regression checks include valid
and incorrect totals with these spellings.

bounded completeness check require rotation plus the illuminated and dark
orientations. Duration and seasonal questions are excluded. A statement about
daylight length does not explain why a place alternates between day and night.
The existing two-attempt ceiling and model settings remain unchanged.

The earlier swap cleanup is retained in `device_cleanup_v1/`: all six existing
swap devices were restored with their original sizes and priorities. Any further
resource preparation and restoration must be recorded separately. Device guard
thresholds remain unchanged. The twenty independently authored cases remain
unopened during this known-case development.
