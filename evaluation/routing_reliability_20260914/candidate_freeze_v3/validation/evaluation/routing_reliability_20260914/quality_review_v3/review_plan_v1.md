# Focused eight-case quality check: frozen independent review plan

This plan is written before the reviewer opens `quality_cases_v3.json`, its case text, labels, or deliverable criteria. The reviewer knows the V2 results and the four affected development cases, and previously contributed safeguards. This is an independent manual assessment of frozen outputs, not an external blind panel. The later eight-case check is distinct from the known affected-case development replay.

Wait for root to freeze the final candidate/source/model inputs and explicitly authorize reading the eight cases. Record the freeze, corpus, configuration, model identities, and review-plan checksums. Only then read the cases and their predeclared deliverable criteria. Do not change those criteria or expectations after seeing outputs, and do not change runtime source, prompts, models, or cases during the check. All eight cases remain in the denominator, including failures or missing outputs.

After the final freeze and explicit start signal, completed rows may be reviewed as they arrive. Findings are quarantined from runtime tuning or model prompts. The final conclusion and durable review are produced only after all eight cases finish, or the run terminates with every unfinished case explicitly accounted for, and cleanup is recorded. No reviewer model calls are planned.

For each case, inspect the exact delivered text against every predeclared deliverable criterion. Preserve a criterion-by-criterion pass/fail/unevaluated record and a concrete reason grounded in the output. A promise, request echo, generic offer, or unnecessary clarification does not satisfy a requested deliverable. Distinguish substantive but partial content from complete content, and weak but sufficient content from an incorrect answer. Check requested counts, formatting, constraints, comparisons, explanations, and draft continuity when the actual case requires them; do not invent additional requirements. If a factual judgment is uncertain, flag it as unevaluated or verify it with a primary source rather than guessing.

Record expected, raw, thresholded, final, and effective dependency modes separately wherever the trace exposes them. Required missing personal information should prompt for that information; optional personalization should fall back to the requested general content when evidence is absent. Mixed tasks must handle their unavailable personal part and deliver the independently answerable general part. An appropriate execution safeguard after a wrong route does not correct the route score.

Independently inspect personal and deployment/action claims regardless of route or model-review verdict. Current assertions and canonical evidence must support the requested subject and value. Task context, fictional draft text, question premises, and optional-personalization instructions are not independent authority for actual personal values. Distinguish unsupported delivered claims, unsupported attempts withheld, and incorrect guard/reviewer accusations. Report finite observations only; this focused check cannot establish universal entailment, lifecycle, or capability guarantees.

Inspect all actual generation attempts and raw answer reviews, including malformed or semantically contradictory results. Separate transport errors, semantic validation failures, model retries, accepted model content, and application-only fallback. Count actual calls against the frozen configured bounds. A model-review pass is not the independent quality judgment. Record admitted context, retrieval/supplied/model/application/response evidence IDs, accepted and withheld attempts, and exact delivered output.

Use one primary delivered form per case: substantive general answer, substantive personal answer, substantive mixed answer, clarification, refusal, unhelpful nonanswer, or execution error/no result. Keep overlapping flags for unnecessary clarification, incorrect memory refusal, missing mixed general content, repetition, promise/echo without delivery, count/format/constraint failure, unsupported personal fact, unsupported deployment/action claim, other factual concern, and incomplete execution.

Report deliverable-criterion completion, independent acceptable behavior, and routing separately, with denominators from the unchanged eight-case corpus. A strict case pass requires its predeclared criteria and applicable routing/evidence behavior; the overall strict check passes only if all eight cases pass. Any unevaluated material criterion prevents an unqualified pass. No favorable aggregate threshold will be selected after observing results. Report generated-delivery latency separately from application fallbacks and note model loading and known concurrency conditions.

At completion, verify archived and recorded run hashes and promptly capture the working-source match before later development resumes. Save exact-output review rows, a machine-readable summary, a readable conclusion, and checksums under this directory. If development is later unfrozen, preserve the original candidate and distinguish later working-tree changes from the completed run. No microphone or speech-recognition claim follows unless the actual trace includes those stages. Once opened, the eight cases are known material and cannot support a later unseen-validation claim.
