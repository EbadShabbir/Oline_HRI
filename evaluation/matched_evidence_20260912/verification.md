# Completion and verification

The [report](README.md) covers all 240 primary attempts on 120 matched requests.
All attempts completed; all raw responses and telemetry remain in [run_v1](run_v1/).
The four excluded harness attempts remain in [smoke_v1](smoke_v1/).

- The runner and analysis passed 24 focused offline tests; the reused device guards
  and telemetry passed 31 tests. These are 55 relevant tests, not a full application
  test-suite claim. Logs: [focused checks](offline_final_validation.txt) and
  [reused infrastructure](reused_infrastructure_validation.txt).
- The independently reviewed dataset, protocol, 39 source files, settings, model
  identities and complete rendered inputs were [frozen before inference](frozen_v1/).
  The [prompt-count audit](prompt_count_audit.json) confirms equal actual prompt
  token counts for all 120 model pairs.
- Two independent blinded assistants each reviewed all 240 answers. All eight
  disagreements were adjudicated before [unblinding](unblinding.json).
  [Frozen reviews](reviews/) and [agreement results](disagreements_v1/agreement.json)
  preserve this record. Independent human validation remains pending.
- An independent auditor recomputed results from frozen raw artifacts without
  importing the main analyzer. All **10,250 checks passed**:
  [numerical audit](final_numeric_audit_v1/numeric_agreement.md).
  A [14-pair spot check](final_numeric_audit_v1/spot_check_notes.md) found no definite
  shared grading defect; this was not a third complete answer review.
- The [resource audit](resource_audit_v1/resource_audit.json) checks device telemetry,
  model residency and preservation of OS configuration. Timing and whole-device
  energy limitations are stated in the report.
- The report [regenerated byte for byte](report_reproduction_check.json) from sealed
  artifacts. [Saved commands](commands.sh) cover validation, collection and analysis.
- The new sections in `results.md` and `developments.MD` preserve all previous text;
  [preservation checks](documentation_preservation.json) record that comparison.

The complete directory is sealed with SHA256 manifests and read-only files and
directories. This provides application-level immutability and tamper detection;
it is not privileged WORM storage. Verify the outer `seal.json` and each nested
`seal.json` when checking integrity. [Completion metadata](completion.json) also
records the nested manifest hashes and final documentation hashes.

These results concern direct generator capability with matched evidence. They do
not establish routing, retrieval, cascade, spoken-system or deployment performance.
