# Representative paired-answer spot check

After authorized unblinding, the independent numerical auditor read the complete
question, evidence, rubric, both answers, and resolved rationale for 14 request pairs:
rg01, rg03, rg04, rg11, cg04, cg12, cg14, cg15, pr01, pr22, pt08, pt12, pt18, pt30.
This is a purposive 28-answer spot check, not a third complete review or human
validation. It covers all four categories and all four paired outcomes.

No definite shared grading defect was established from these examples. The checked
decisions preserve unfavorable outcomes as well as useful rescues. Examples include
both models' incorrect bowl arithmetic (rg04), the only-small success and adjudicated
unsupported basket alternative (rg11), both models reversing the book/dust sequence
(cg12), the larger model's inclusion of a gift outside the requested interval (pt08),
and its inclusive total of four instead of the two intervening jigsaws (pt12).

The larger model's wording about folding laundry "for storage or washing" (rg03)
is awkward; the response still supplies the required distinction between grouping
laundry and folding individual items. This spot check does not establish a material
rubric violation warranting a correction. Judgments were left unchanged.

All frozen reviews remain intact. Numerical agreement is conditional on those
assistant judgments. Independent human validation of the authored workload and
answer grading remains pending; no application, router, cascade, or spoken-system
accuracy result is established by this component experiment.
